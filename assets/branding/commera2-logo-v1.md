## Generation Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset: a new primary logo for Commera2, a modern ecommerce management application used by independent, nontechnical shop owners.
Create ONE refined, original, professional horizontal logo lockup: a compact distinctive symbol on the left and the wordmark on the right. Not a contact sheet, not a brand presentation.
Symbol concept: a bold geometric C built from two precisely balanced interlocking forms, with a small forward-stepping cut suggesting movement and connected commerce. Open negative space, softly squared curves, very simple silhouette, optically balanced. It must remain recognizable at small app-icon sizes. Do not put it inside an additional decorative square. Avoid generic shopping carts, shopping bags, globes, rockets, swooshes or stock chain-link icons.
Typography: exact text "Commera2", capital C then ommera then numeral 2, on one baseline. Clear custom-feeling contemporary sans-serif, medium/semibold, not futuristic, not extremely rounded, not tightly condensed. Meticulous spacing. Wordmark darker than symbol; the 2 is full-size and integrated, not superscript.
Palette: flat deep teal #087F83 and deep evergreen #173E34 for the two simple symbol forms; charcoal #202B32 for the wordmark, with the 2 in teal. Restrained, crisp, businesslike but approachable.
Composition: wide landscape approximately 3:1, centered lockup with generous clean transparent margin, icon about 1.25 times cap-height, measured space between icon and name.
Output: genuinely transparent PNG background with clean sharp opaque logo edges. Flat vector-like artwork, suitable for later vector production. No gradients, shadow, bevel, embossing, texture, 3D, mockup, paper, decorative background, extra words, tagline, watermark, registration mark or repeated logo.
